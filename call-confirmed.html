Before we talk

One thing to think about.

This isn't a pitch. It's a real conversation about why your best tennis shows up in practice and goes missing the moment the score matters.

So before the call, think of one specific match where that happened to you. A match you should have won, against someone you know you can beat. That single match is usually enough for Benoit to start seeing the pattern.

If you have a short video of yourself playing, bring that too and you'll watch it together on the call. In the meantime, the Learn articles are a good place to start.
